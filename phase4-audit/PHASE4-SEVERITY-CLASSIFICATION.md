- Critical: exposure or failure of a production security boundary (the recorded NDASH-007).
- High: materially wrong financial totals, reporting periods or office attribution; fabricated financial categories; misleading ingestion/data-integrity status; incorrect payroll/person scope.
- Medium: broken search/navigation/refresh, incomplete administrative listings, misleading nonfinancial status or attribution labels, and controls that do not carry out their stated function without the High impacts above.
- Low: cosmetic/wording issues classified Low in the retained issue records.
